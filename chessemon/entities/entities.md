
# Chessemon Entities

## Chessemon Pieces

Each Chessemon has the following stats:
- **HP** (Hit Points): How much damage it can take before being captured.
- **ATK** (Attack): Damage dealt in combat.
- **MOV** (Movement): How it moves on the board.
- **Ability**: Unique effect or passive.

### Kingmon
- HP: 3 | ATK: 1 | MOV: 1 square any direction (like a chess King)
- Ability: "Royal Command" – Once per game, move any friendly Chessemon as if it were a Queenmon.

### Pawnmon
- HP: 1 | ATK: 1 | MOV: 1 square forward (like a chess Pawn)
- Ability: "Eager" – May move two squares forward on its first move.
- Evolution: Can evolve into Knightmon, Bishopmon, Rookmon, or Queenmon with the appropriate Evolution Card.

### Knightmon
- HP: 2 | ATK: 2 | MOV: L-shape (like a chess Knight)
- Ability: "Leap" – Ignores other pieces when moving.

### Bishopmon
- HP: 2 | ATK: 2 | MOV: Diagonal any distance (like a chess Bishop)
- Ability: "Heal" – Once per game, restore 1 HP to any friendly Chessemon.

### Rookmon
- HP: 2 | ATK: 2 | MOV: Orthogonal any distance (like a chess Rook)
- Ability: "Fortify" – Gains +1 HP while on a Home Zone.

### Queenmon
- HP: 3 | ATK: 3 | MOV: Any direction, any distance (like a chess Queen)
- Ability: "Overwhelm" – May attack twice per turn.

## Card Types & Examples

### Move Card Example
- **Dash**: Move a Chessemon up to 2 extra squares this turn.
- **Strike**: Attack an adjacent enemy for +1 ATK this turn.

### Evolution Card Example
- **Evolve to Knightmon**: Evolve a Pawnmon into Knightmon.
- **Evolve to Queenmon**: Evolve a Pawnmon into Queenmon.

### Item Card Example
- **Potion**: Restore 2 HP to any Chessemon.
- **Shield**: Prevent all damage to a Chessemon this turn.

## Board Zones
- **Home Zone**: 2x4 area at each player's board edge; starting area for Chessemon.
- **Battle Zone**: 4x4 central area; most combat occurs here.
- **Bench**: Off-board area for captured or benched Chessemon.

## States
- **Active**: On the board and able to act.
- **Benched**: Off the board, can be swapped in (using a card or ability).
- **Evolved**: Upgraded form with new stats and abilities.

## Example Team Setup
- 1 Kingmon, 6 Pawnmon per player (standard)

## Example Deck
- 10 Move Cards, 10 Item Cards, 10 Evolution Cards (customizable)
