# Chessemon Official Rules

## Objective
Defeat your opponent by capturing their Kingmon or fulfilling a special win condition specified by card effects.

## Setup
- Each player selects a team of Chessemon and builds a deck of 30 cards.
- Place Chessemon on the Home Zone; draw 5 cards to form your starting hand.

### Board Layout
```
  [Home Zone] [Battle Zone] [Home Zone]
  | 2x4      |   4x4      | 2x4   |
```
Each player’s Home Zone is at opposite board edges. Place 1 Kingmon and 6 Pawnmon in your Home Zone at the start.

## Gameplay
1. Players alternate turns, following the Turn Structure (see Mechanics).
2. On your turn, you may move Chessemon, play cards, attack, or evolve.
3. Captured Chessemon are sent to the Bench; evolved forms revert to base if captured.
4. Special win conditions may be triggered by certain cards (e.g., control all Battle Zones).

### Card Clarifications
- **Move Cards**: Play during your Action Phase to grant extra movement or special attacks.
- **Evolution Cards**: Play on a Pawnmon to evolve it into a higher form. Replace the piece, keep any damage.
- **Item Cards**: Play at any time during your Action Phase for healing, buffs, or protection.

### Combat
- Declare attacks with Active Chessemon. Attacks target adjacent enemies unless a card/ability says otherwise.
- Damage is subtracted from HP. If HP reaches 0, the Chessemon is captured and sent to the Bench.

### Benched Chessemon
- Benched Chessemon may be swapped in using certain cards or abilities.

### Evolving
- Each Pawnmon may only evolve once per turn.
- Evolved Chessemon retain any damage taken.

### Special Abilities
- Each Chessemon has a unique ability (see entities.md). Abilities may be passive or activated.

### Example Win Conditions
- Capture the opponent’s Kingmon.
- Control all Battle Zone squares for a full turn (if a card effect allows).

### Advanced Rules
- If both players have only Kingmon left, the game is a draw after 3 more turns.
- If a player cannot draw a card at the start of their turn, they lose.

## Restrictions
- Only one Evolution per Pawnmon per turn.
- Maximum hand size: 7 cards (discard down at end of turn).
- No duplicate Kingmon on a team.

## End of Game
- The game ends when a Kingmon is captured or a special win condition is met.
