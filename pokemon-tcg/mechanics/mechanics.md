# Game Mechanics Reference

## Mechanics List

### Deck Construction
- **Description:** Players build a 60-card deck from their collection, choosing Pokémon, Trainer, and Energy cards to create strategic combinations.
- **BGG Mapping:** Deck Construction
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** 2001
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** "Players construct their own deck of cards prior to the game, often from a larger pool of cards."
- **Justification:** Standard CCG mechanic; directly matches BGG.

### Hand Management
- **Description:** Players draw, hold, and play cards from their hand, making decisions about which cards to use and when.
- **BGG Mapping:** Hand Management
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** 2040
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** "Players must manage the cards in their hand, making choices about which to play and which to hold."
- **Justification:** Standard CCG mechanic; directly matches BGG.

### Open Drafting
- **Description:** Players may draft cards from a shared pool in some formats (not core rules, but supported in official play).
- **BGG Mapping:** Open Drafting
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** 2003
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** "Players take turns selecting cards from a common pool."
- **Justification:** Not in core rules, but present in official tournament formats.

### Variable Player Powers
- **Description:** Each Pokémon card has unique attacks and abilities, giving players different powers based on their deck.
- **BGG Mapping:** Variable Player Powers
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** 2017
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** "Each player has different abilities or powers."
- **Justification:** Each deck and Pokémon is unique; matches BGG.

### Prize Card System
- **Description:** Players set aside six Prize cards at the start. When a Pokémon is Knocked Out, the opponent takes a Prize card. The first to take all Prize cards wins.
- **BGG Mapping:** Unique
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** N/A
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** N/A
- **Justification:** No direct BGG equivalent; unique to Pokémon TCG.

### Knock Out/Elimination
- **Description:** When a Pokémon's damage equals or exceeds its HP, it is Knocked Out and sent to the discard pile.
- **BGG Mapping:** Player Elimination (partial), but more accurately "Unit Elimination"
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** 2010 (Player Elimination)
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** "Players are eliminated from the game when certain conditions are met."
- **Justification:** Pokémon, not players, are eliminated; partial match.

### Resource Management (Energy Attachment)
- **Description:** Players attach Energy cards to Pokémon to pay for attacks and abilities, managing limited resources each turn.
- **BGG Mapping:** Resource Management
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** 2025
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** "Players must manage resources to perform actions."
- **Justification:** Standard CCG mechanic; matches BGG.

### Card Play/Action Selection
- **Description:** Players choose actions each turn: play Pokémon, evolve, attach Energy, play Trainer cards, retreat, use Abilities, and attack.
- **BGG Mapping:** Action Points (partial), Card Play Conflict Resolution
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** 2042 (Action Points), 2043 (Card Play Conflict Resolution)
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** "Players have a set number of actions per turn." / "Players play cards to resolve conflicts."
- **Justification:** Turn structure is action-based; partial match.

### Evolution
- **Description:** Players evolve Pokémon by placing Evolution cards on top of Basic Pokémon, gaining new abilities and attacks.
- **BGG Mapping:** Unique
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** N/A
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** N/A
- **Justification:** No direct BGG equivalent; unique to Pokémon TCG.

### Special Conditions
- **Description:** Status effects (Asleep, Burned, Confused, Paralyzed, Poisoned) alter Pokémon behavior and persist until cured.
- **BGG Mapping:** Unique
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** N/A
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** N/A
- **Justification:** No direct BGG equivalent; unique to Pokémon TCG.

### Retreat
- **Description:** Players may switch their Active Pokémon with a Benched Pokémon by paying the Retreat Cost.
- **BGG Mapping:** Unique
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** N/A
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** N/A
- **Justification:** No direct BGG equivalent; unique to Pokémon TCG.

### Zones/Board Management
- **Description:** The game uses distinct zones: Active Spot, Bench, Deck, Discard Pile, Hand, Prize Cards.
- **BGG Mapping:** Area Movement (partial)
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** 2006
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** "Players move pieces between areas."
- **Justification:** Partial match; zones are static, but cards move between them.

### Sudden Death
- **Description:** If both players win simultaneously, a one-Prize-card game is played to determine the winner.
- **BGG Mapping:** Tiebreaker (partial)
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** N/A
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** N/A
- **Justification:** No direct BGG equivalent; tiebreaker is a common concept.

### Card Drawing
- **Description:** Players draw cards at the start of their turn and as directed by card effects.
- **BGG Mapping:** Card Drafting (partial), Hand Management
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** 2040
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** "Players must manage the cards in their hand."
- **Justification:** Standard CCG mechanic; matches BGG.

### Mulligan
- **Description:** If a player has no Basic Pokémon in their opening hand, they reveal, reshuffle, and redraw, with the opponent drawing extra cards for each mulligan.
- **BGG Mapping:** Unique
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** N/A
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** N/A
- **Justification:** No direct BGG equivalent; unique to Pokémon TCG.

### Abilities
- **Description:** Some Pokémon have special Abilities that can be used during a turn, separate from attacks.
- **BGG Mapping:** Variable Player Powers
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** 2017
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** "Each player has different abilities or powers."
- **Justification:** Matches BGG; implemented via cards.

### Attack Resolution
- **Description:** Attacks require specific Energy, may have effects, and deal damage, which is modified by Weakness/Resistance.
- **BGG Mapping:** Dice Rolling (partial), Variable Player Powers
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** 2017
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** "Each player has different abilities or powers."
- **Justification:** Attacks are deterministic but may involve coin flips.

### Coin Flipping/Randomizer
- **Description:** Many effects require flipping a coin to determine outcomes (e.g., Special Conditions, attack effects).
- **BGG Mapping:** Randomness, Dice Rolling (partial)
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** 2023
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** "Players roll dice to determine outcomes."
- **Justification:** Coin flips are a form of randomizer; partial match.

### Card Types (Pokémon, Trainer, Energy)
- **Description:** The game uses three main card types, each with unique rules and functions.
- **BGG Mapping:** Unique
- **BGG Mechanic ID(s):** N/A
- **BGG Mechanic Description(s):** N/A
- **Justification:** No direct BGG equivalent; unique to Pokémon TCG.
