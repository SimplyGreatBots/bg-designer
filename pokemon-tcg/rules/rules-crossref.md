### Deck Construction (Article 1.1)
- **Rule:** Each player must build a deck of exactly 60 cards, with no more than 4 cards of the same name (except Basic Energy), and at least one Basic Pokémon.
- **Context:** Ensures balanced gameplay and strategic deck-building.
- **Related Mechanic(s):** [Deck Construction](../mechanics/mechanics.md#deck-construction)
- **Example:** "Your deck must be exactly 60 cards; it can’t be any more or less, even 59 or 61 cards! ...except for Basic Energy, you can only have 4 cards with the same name in your deck."

### Hand Management (Article 1.2)
- **Rule:** Players draw, hold, and play cards from their hand, making decisions about which cards to use and when. Each player draws 7 cards at the start and keeps their hand hidden.
- **Context:** Core to turn structure and resource management.
- **Related Mechanic(s):** [Hand Management](../mechanics/mechanics.md#hand-management)

### Prize Card System (Article 1.3)
- **Rule:** Each player sets aside 6 Prize cards at the start. When a Pokémon is Knocked Out, the opponent takes a Prize card. The first to take all Prize cards wins.
- **Context:** Unique win condition for Pokémon TCG.
- **Related Mechanic(s):** [Prize Card System](../mechanics/mechanics.md#prize-card-system)

### Knock Out/Elimination (Article 1.4)
- **Rule:** When a Pokémon has damage equal to or greater than its HP, it is Knocked Out and sent to the discard pile. The opponent takes a Prize card.
- **Context:** Drives the flow of battle and win conditions.
- **Related Mechanic(s):** [Knock Out/Elimination](../mechanics/mechanics.md#knock-outelimination)

### Resource Management (Energy Attachment) (Article 1.5)

### Retreat (Article 1.9)
